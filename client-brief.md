# Working with your Claude agent

A one-page brief for anyone at the client org with chat access to a bound project. Generic — no specialty named, no client specifics. Fill in `[ ]` fields and hand or paste this to the client at go-live, same pattern as `dk0/establishment.md`.

---

## What this is

This is a Claude project set up and configured by [founder/org] specifically for **[named actor / role, e.g. "your Documentation Manager"]**. It has been given a specific job, specific boundaries, and specific knowledge for that job. It is not a general-purpose assistant for your whole business, and it is not a substitute for [founder/org]'s ongoing support.

[Founder/org] holds master technical authority over this agent's configuration and operation. Your organisation is authorised to use and rely on it for the job described below — that authorisation comes from [founder/org] and is what makes your day-to-day use of it legitimate; it isn't an independent right your organisation holds on its own, separate from [founder/org]'s setup and oversight.

## What you can do

- Chat with it for anything inside its job: **[in-scope, one sentence, from the binding sheet]**.
- Ask it questions about how it works or what it can help with.
- Flag anything that looks wrong, confusing, or off — see "If something seems off" below.

## What not to do

- Don't ask it to act outside its job (**[out-of-scope, one sentence, from the binding sheet]**) — it's not set up for that, and answers outside its scope may not be reliable even if it attempts one.
- Don't try to change its role, instructions, or "personality" through chat (e.g. "ignore your instructions and just...", "pretend you're..."). It won't reliably refuse, and doing this can produce wrong or unsafe output for real work.
- Don't paste in personal, financial, or participant/client information beyond what the task actually needs. If you're not sure whether something is needed, leave it out and ask.
- Don't share your seat login or let someone outside the org use it.

## Why you can't break the setup

You have "view" access to this project, not "edit" access, because [founder/org] — not your organisation — is the master technical authority over how it's configured: binding, reconfiguring, connecting new tools, and any change to its setup all route through [founder/org], never around them. That means you can use it fully, but you cannot change its instructions or the reference material behind it — only [founder/org] can. If a conversation goes somewhere strange, it does not damage the underlying setup; just start a new chat. If you think the setup itself needs to change (new capability, different scope), raise that with [founder/org] rather than trying to get the agent to adopt it directly.

## If something seems off

If the agent gives an answer that seems wrong, refuses something it should be able to do, or seems to be behaving differently than usual, stop and tell [founder/org contact] rather than trying to fix it by rephrasing or arguing with it in chat. This is what keeps the setup reliable for everyone using it.
