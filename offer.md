# Offer — what the shopfront words mean

Shopfront hangs off HQ as a separate public surface. Grey. Not the asset library. Not a client system. The live page is `jyoperatives/personal-ops`, not this repo.

This card is the missing HQ half: **definitions**. [`claims.md`](claims.md) traces a published sentence back to a process. It cannot do that while the words themselves have no meaning. [`factory.md`](factory.md) is how a client buys and onboards. This file is what JYOps is allowed to mean when it says the thing out loud.

Not a marketing rewrite. No pricing. Do not invent statute text.

The split that every term below has to survive:

- **JYOps process outcome** — documentation, records, and billing trail in a state that can survive audit. This is the [NDIS auditing](specialties/ndis-auditing.md) specialty. Readiness and evidence hygiene. Not running the provider.
- **Commission / auditor outcome** — whether a registration is granted, a practice audit is passed, a finding is issued. JYOps does not issue that decision and must not talk as if it does.

If a live line collapses those two, it is not defined. It is a liability.

The terms below were transcribed from the supplied landing-page repository's written guarantee page. They describe what is currently published. Founder approval is still required before HQ marks the terms as adopted and treats them as the controlled offer.

---

## 1. Registration-ready

**Published line:** "Unregistered provider to registration-ready in less than a week."

**Means:** the in-scope documentation, records, and billing trail are in a state the founder will put in front of an auditor. Same test as the specialty: survive audit, not run the company.

**Does not mean:** the NDIS Commission (or any auditor) has registered the provider, approved an application, or scheduled an audit. JYOps does not sell a Commission decision.

**In-scope for the week** is whatever the binding sheet named at recon. Typical pair: Documentation Manager + Invoice / Billing Manager. Not PBS. Not "fifty functions." Not modules that were out of scope.

**Clock — current published term:**

| Beat | Event |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Calendar days begin when registration groups, organisation details, worker details and screening status, and access to existing documents have been supplied. |
| Pause | Time waiting on the client, screening body, or Commission is not counted. |
| Scope | The registration groups applied for, as supplied at clock start. |
| Length | Less than one week, measured in calendar days under the published exclusions. |

If the week is missed due only to JYOps delay, the published term says that month's fee is waived. Teams of five or more employees, client delay, screening delay, Commission delay, and withheld or inaccurate information are excluded.

---

## 2. Audit-pass guarantee

**Published line:** 100% audit pass or your money back; next audit on me.

**"Pass" — current published term:** no non-conformity that prevents registration or renewal. Minor corrective items alone are not a fail.

Out of this promise:

- The Commission's overall registration decision.
- Worker screening, incidents, or practice the provider did not fix when told.
- Modules that were out of scope on the binding sheet.
- A client who ignored [`client-brief.md`](client-brief.md), withheld evidence, or changed structure without re-establishment.

**Money back — current published term:** all JYOps fees paid for the audit-preparation period up to the audit date, refunded within 14 days of the written outcome if the guarantee fails. The claim is made in writing; the published page gives a three-business-day reply expectation.

**Next audit on me — current published term:** remediation of the non-conformity, re-preparation of files, and support through the re-audit until the next outcome, at no additional fee. The approved-quality-auditor's invoice is excluded unless agreed in writing.

**Window — current published term:** first registration audit is covered from 30 days after engagement; other covered audits from 7 days after engagement. The audit date must be supplied in writing at least 14 days before it.

Covered audits are first registration, mid-term, renewal, and practice audits against the NDIS Practice Standards. The published exclusions include teams of five or more employees, withheld or inaccurate information, undisclosed pre-existing matters, worker or service-delivery conduct, unaddressed corrective items, and regulatory action already on foot at engagement.

## 3. 24/7 expert support

**Published line:** live NDIS-expert support, 24/7 / me on the line.

**Means:** the live shopfront provides a chat channel with an NDIS expert. “24/7” means the support channel is available continuously; it is not a staffed call-centre promise.

**Does not mean:** a 15-minute phone SLA, a second named practitioner, or PBS clinical coverage.

**Response — current published term:** visitors can talk live; if the founder misses them, they leave the message in the same chat. Written guarantee claims receive a reply within five business days. This does not create a general first-response SLA for ordinary chat.

---

## 4. Overheads down

**Published line:** "Your overheads go down. Guaranteed."

**Means:** hours per fortnight spent across claiming, invoicing, file maintenance, and audit preparation go down during the first 90 days after migration completes.

**Baseline:** recorded jointly at onboarding, before migration. If no joint baseline is recorded, this guarantee does not run.

**If the measure does not improve:** fees for that measurement period are refunded. Teams of five or more employees, no joint baseline, and withheld or inaccurate hours are excluded.

---

## 5. Data safety, backup, and compliant handling

**Published line:** "Guaranteed data safety, backup, and compliant handling of your records."

**Means:** organisation and staff records, participant records and service documentation, claiming and invoice data, policies, and registers are hosted on Supabase under the published NDIS-appropriate handling model. JYOps and named subprocessors may access provider records for the service; no other purpose is stated.

**Backup:** daily. Restore is best effort within 24 hours of a written request.

**Incident and privacy term:** written notice within 24 hours of JYOps becoming aware of a break; the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Notifiable Data Breaches scheme apply. These are published terms, not an independent compliance certification.

---

## 6. Founder qualification

**Means:** discovery call is a qualification gate, not a courtesy. Almost always is not always.

**Decline** `[founder confirm]` — current HQ proposal (not located as a written landing-page term):

- Not an NDIS provider, or not becoming one.
- Will not provision their own Claude Team plan.
- Wants edit access on a bound project.
- Wants the PBS pack as the product (utilisation not chosen; not approved as a build).
- Will not accept [`client-brief.md`](client-brief.md) / view-only.
- Recon shows in-scope work JYOps does not yet have a shelf asset for, and the founder will not invent one on the call.

Until this list is confirmed, qualification is judgment-only.
