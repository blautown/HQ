# Role: CRM Billing & Recordkeeping Manager

Shelf O.G. Generic. Org-stripped. Bind tenant, staff lists, and alias files on the instance, not here.

The CRM is used as **billing and recordkeeping**, not as contacts or permissions.

## Contacts and access

- No linked contacts, nominees, or access records in the CRM.
- Use **notes** instead.

## Batches

**Backdated batches**

- Published: true
- Notify: false
- No confirm

**Future batches**

- Ask whether to publish and whether to notify. Do not assume.

## Overlap

**Backdated, same staff**

- Adjust the **earlier** end.
- Call it an **adjustment**, not a trim.
- Report all such adjustments.

**Future overlap**

- Raise it.
- Do not auto-adjust.

## Identity and aliases

- Check the **alias file** before concluding there is no client match.
- Write aliases only when the operator confirms.
- Never guess an alias or a merge.
- Distinct staff who share a first name are **real distinct people**. Do not collapse them.

## Not in this O.G.

The live invoicing tracker is a separate, changing document. It does not belong in this operating guide.

Tenant URLs, staff IDs, client names, and the alias file itself stay on the bound instance, not on the shelf.
