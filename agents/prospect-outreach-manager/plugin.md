# DK:1 plugin — NDIS outreach compliance framing

Read [`../dk0/domain-knowledge-boundaries.md`](../../dk0/domain-knowledge-boundaries.md) first if you haven't.

Clips onto [`identity.md`](identity.md) (mirror of `dk0/prospect-outreach-manager.md`). Does not restate its process rules — session gate, scope lock, and escalation thresholds stay on that card; this plugin only adds domain content on top.

Org-stripped. No client data, no participant files, no live prospect lists.

## What this adds

NDIS provider vocabulary and roles, so drafted outreach copy reads as informed rather than generic:

- Registered NDIS provider
- Support Coordinator
- Plan Manager
- Supported Independent Living (SIL) provider
- Local Area Coordinator (LAC)
- Plan-managed vs. agency-managed vs. self-managed participant funding

## Cited domain content

Source: *NDIS Compliance and Enforcement Framework* (`ndis.gov.au/media/2420/download`), §4.1.1 "Education and Outreach":

> "The Agency uses targeted education and outreach campaigns to provide information about responsibilities, compliance obligations and the Agency's approach to compliance... The Agency considers prevention of a breach always preferable to taking action after a breach has occurred."

This was confirmed via two independent search cross-references (including the neighbouring §4.1.2 "Industry Engagement" heading, which corroborates the numbering), not a direct read of the PDF — direct fetch returned a 403. Re-verify against the live document itself before this plugin is relied on for a client-facing claim.

This citation informs **messaging credibility** only: it lets outreach copy frame itself around a provider's own proactive-compliance posture (prevention preferred over enforcement), echoing language the Agency itself uses. It is not legal permission for this asset to solicit, and it does not relax any of `identity.md`'s email-compliance or scope rules.

If an actual outreach-conduct restriction specific to NDIS providers is needed later (for example, a rule limiting how or when a provider may be contacted), that is a separate, currently-unheld citation. Do not invent it — state that it is missing until a real source is found.

## Not in this card

Live prospect lists, contact details, participant data, or any org's actual compliance history. No specialty content beyond the vocabulary and the one citation above.
